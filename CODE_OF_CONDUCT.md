# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, considerate, and inclusive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, threats, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
